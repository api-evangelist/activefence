# Alice

> Alice provides AI security, safety, and trust across every application, agent, and model, from build through production.

Alice, formerly ActiveFence, helps enterprises and frontier AI labs test, protect, and evaluate AI systems against adversarial attacks, prompt injection, jailbreaks, unsafe outputs, policy violations, data leakage, model drift, and emerging risks.

Alice operates across two primary areas:

- WonderSuite: an enterprise AI security and governance platform for testing, protecting, and monitoring AI applications and agents.
- Alice Labs: frontier research, adversarial evaluation, datasets, detection signals, and agentic environments for foundation-model developers and AI labs.

Alice is headquartered in New York, United States, and Tel Aviv, Israel. Alice is SOC 2 certified and ISO 27001 certified.

## WonderSuite

- [WonderSuite platform](https://alice.io/products/platform): Alice's enterprise AI security and governance platform for pre-launch stress testing, real-time runtime protection, and ongoing production evaluation.
- [WonderBuild](https://alice.io/products/wonderbuild): Automated and managed pre-deployment red teaming for AI models, applications, and agents. WonderBuild helps identify prompt injection, jailbreaks, unsafe behavior, data leakage, policy violations, and other adversarial vulnerabilities before launch.
- [WonderFence](https://alice.io/products/wonderfence): Dynamic runtime guardrails and policy enforcement for live AI applications and agents. WonderFence evaluates prompts and responses, blocks harmful inputs and outputs, and applies controls aligned to business requirements, compliance obligations, brand standards, and risk tolerance.
- [WonderCheck](https://alice.io/products/wondercheck): Ongoing post-launch red teaming and production evaluation for detecting drift, regressions, emerging vulnerabilities, and changes introduced by model, prompt, workflow, or system updates.

WonderSuite is designed to provide one coordinated layer for testing before deployment, protection in production, and continuous evaluation as AI systems evolve.

## Enterprise AI applications and agents

- [GenAI apps and agents](https://alice.io/solutions/apps-and-agents): Security, safety, and governance for customer-facing AI applications, chatbots, copilots, autonomous agents, and agentic workflows.

Alice helps enterprises address:

- Prompt injection and jailbreaks
- Unsafe or misleading outputs
- Data leakage and PII exposure
- Tool misuse and unauthorized actions
- Policy and regulatory violations
- Model, prompt, and behavioral drift
- Brand-alignment failures
- Application-level exploitation
- Multi-turn and multilingual attacks
- Risks created by third-party models, platforms, and agent frameworks

## Control for CX agents

Alice provides lifecycle-wide control for customer-experience AI, including AI chatbots, agentic AI, and consumer-facing applications.

- Pre-launch testing helps identify failures in refund handling, promotions, account access, escalation, policy adherence, safety, and brand behavior before customers encounter them.
- Runtime guardrails enforce company-specific policies during live interactions.
- Ongoing evaluation detects drift, regressions, and emerging risks after deployment.
- Application-level monitoring provides visibility into prompts, outputs, tool calls, agent actions, and unexpected behavior.

- [CX agent security and control](https://alice.io/solutions/apps-and-agents): Learn how WonderSuite supports customer-facing AI and agentic workflows.
- [The Harm In Not Knowing What Your CX Agent Is Saying](https://alice.io/blog/the-harm-in-not-knowing-what-your-cx-agent-is-saying): Alice perspective on the operational, security, compliance, and brand risks created by customer-facing AI agents.

## Alice Labs and foundation models

- [Alice Labs and foundation-model solutions](https://alice.io/solutions/foundation-models): Frontier research, adversarial evaluation, training data, detection signals, and agentic environments for foundation-model developers and AI labs.

Alice Labs supports foundation-model development through:

- Training datasets for model, application, and agent training
- Expert-led and automated evaluations and red teaming
- Detection signals for guardrails and content classifiers
- Agentic reinforcement-learning environments
- Multimodal testing across text, image, audio, speech, and video
- Multilingual and cross-cultural research
- Safety, security, privacy, bias, deception, child safety, CBRNE, financial, legal, and medical risk evaluation
- Testing of model, infrastructure, ecosystem, and agentic attack surfaces

Alice produces human-created, synthetically generated, and real-world datasets suitable for supervised fine-tuning, reinforcement learning from human feedback, evaluation, benchmarking, guardrail development, and classifier training.

## Rabbit Hole adversarial intelligence

- [Rabbit Hole](https://alice.io/intelligence): Alice's proprietary adversarial intelligence engine, built from nearly a decade of global trust, safety, and security research.

Rabbit Hole is informed by billions of real-world toxic, deceptive, manipulative, abusive, and adversarial samples across more than 120 languages and cultural contexts.

Rabbit Hole supports Alice by:

- Generating realistic adversarial scenarios
- Identifying emerging misuse and attack techniques
- Improving classifiers and guardrails
- Supporting multilingual and multimodal coverage
- Informing pre-deployment testing and ongoing production evaluation
- Helping teams test against how systems fail in practice, not only in theory
